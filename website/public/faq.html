FAQ

Questions before take-off

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, never. It finds, scores, drafts and fills. Submitting is always your click, after you've checked the form.

Which jobs does it cover?

Any role, across Europe. It starts from a shared list of employers' own job pages, checked daily and growing in every field, plus job boards. You choose your roles and places; a search only shows jobs that match them. See the numbers.

Do I need Notion or any other account?

No. By default your jobs and applications stay in the app on your computer. Notion (your own cloud, if you want your data there), Gmail, Telegram and GitHub are optional extras you can connect later.

What does the AI cost?

Free AI is included at no cost, within the free allowance; it is a bit weaker than Pro AI. Pro uses smarter in-house AI while you have credit. If you add your own Claude or ChatGPT key, the cost goes to you directly and you can see it: each run shows what it cost, and a monthly budget you set caps it.

Is it really free? What happens after the free period?

Free for your first 20 applications or your first 60 days, whichever lasts longer. After that the app pauses only new work (preparing kits, filling forms, searching); tracking, export and your data keep working, and it never stops in the middle of an application. Pro, coming soon, is a one-time payment that removes the limit: Pro €9, or Pro + AI €19.

What will Pro cost?

A one-time payment, no subscription (coming soon): Pro €9, or Pro + AI €19. Both remove the application limit and let you export your job list as a CSV or JSON file; Pro + AI includes €10 of AI credit (about 70 applications); top up with €5 (about 35 applications), €10 (about 70) or €20 (about 140), each adding what you pay if you search heavily, or use your own Claude or ChatGPT key, which is unlimited. 14-day refund, any reason. Join the waitlist.

Mac, Windows or Linux?

Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and Windows 10 or 11 (x64, beta). Windows installs from the Microsoft Store, with no warning; the Mac app isn't signed by Apple yet, so your Mac asks once before opening it. Filling forms works in Chrome and Edge. Linux: run it from the source. All supported platforms.

What happens to my CV?

Your CV file is stored only on your computer, even with hosted Always on. The AI reads it to draft your profile (which you review) and, when you ask, to make a version tailored to one job; with Free AI that text passes through our server to the model and is not kept. You see every change before it's used. Nothing is stored on a Jobadabra server unless you switch on hosted Always on, which keeps your profile encrypted.

Can I add my own employers?

Yes. Add any company on the Employers page and it is checked with the shared list, or turn on your own scout to find more. Your list stays yours and is never uploaded. By default the app shares which employers' job pages it reads, to help the shared list grow; you can switch that off in Settings (details).

Does it work with LinkedIn?

It doesn't read or post on LinkedIn, but you can paste or screenshot a recruiter's conversation and it is tracked. Most roles you'd find there are also on the employer's own job page, which is where it looks and where you apply.

How is it different from other job tools?

It does the whole search, not one step: finds and scores jobs, drafts each form's answers, tailors your CV, fills the form and tracks replies, with your data on your computer, in your own Notion, or encrypted on our servers: you choose. It never mass-applies. See the side-by-side comparison.

Can I run it myself for free?

Yes. The code is public for now, so you can read it and run it from source. The apps are free to start too.
