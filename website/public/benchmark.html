    What these numbers can tell you

    These are benchmarks we run ourselves, not an independent certification or a promise about every job site.

    Blind judging
    The judge sees the answer or listing and the supplied facts, without the tool's name. Search verdicts separate role and location; form verdicts separate right, wrong and empty.
    Search limits
    Different source coverage and small samples matter. Role match is not application success. “Both” requires role yes and place yes; remote and unknown location verdicts are kept separate. These rounds do not measure all jobs available on the market.
    Form limits
    One repeat per tool, a handful of forms, one fictional applicant and Haiku 5.5 on CI. Model access and tool execution differ. A single repeat gives no estimate of run-to-run variation. It is not a production-wide success rate.
    Which round is shown
    We review every round in full first, then publish the one worth showing. The page shows the latest round we published, with its date; an older one stays out of sight until we choose it again.
    Check the evidence
    The downloadable snapshot contains aggregate counts, fictional persona labels, the scored run ID and source hashes. It contains no applicant answers or personal data. This is a dated snapshot, not a live counter.
