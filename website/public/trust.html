Trust

Why you can trust the download

You don't have to take our word for it: the code is public for now and every download can be checked.

  • 📖 Code public for nowWhat it reads, what it sends, where your keys go. Read it on GitHub
  • 🏗️ Built in publicEach download is built by GitHub Actions from a tagged commit, not on anyone's laptop. The build logs are public.
  • ✅ CheckableGitHub signs each download with the commit it was built from. With the GitHub CLI: gh attestation verify Jobadabra-mac-arm64.dmg -R GarryOne/jobadabra
  • 🔒 What reaches our serversBy default, nothing about you: it stays on your computer, or in your own Notion if you choose that. AI: with Free AI a request passes through our server to the model and nothing is stored; with your own key the AI runs from your computer. Hosted Always on (optional) stores what it needs, encrypted. E-mail alerts you chose pass through our server and wait at most 14 days for your digest. Technical reports are scrubbed, and you can turn them off.
  • ✋ You submitIt fills forms and never presses Submit or applies in bulk.
  • ⚠️ Not signed by Apple yetThat's why a Mac asks once before opening it. On Windows it comes from the Microsoft Store, checked by Microsoft. Found a problem? Report it privately to contact@jobadabra.top
