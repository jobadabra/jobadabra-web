Along the way

It keeps up, and it learns

  • Your e-mail, checked

    Connect Gmail and a reply moves its job to Interview or Rejected by itself. Read-only.

  • What to do next

    AI suggestions on Focus and Jobs, and a look at your week.

  • It learns about you

    An answer you give once fills the next form. The jobs you keep, dismiss and apply to shape what comes next.

It fills the form

Your answers, in seconds

The browser extension (Chrome or Edge) fills the employer's form from your CV and your answers, and the app follows every field. Anything it doesn't know, it asks you once.

Everything the form asks
Contact details, your CV, a cover letter and every question, from your application kit

Answer once

Never guesses
Anything uncertain is marked for you to check
